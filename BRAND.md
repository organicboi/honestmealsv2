# Honest Meals — Brand Document

---

## What We Are

**Honest Meals** is a Pune-based food and fitness platform built for people who take what they eat seriously.

We are not a generic food delivery app. We are a full health stack — meals you can trust, tools to track every number, and a workout log to prove your work. Everything in one place, nothing hidden.

---

## The Core Problem We Solve

Most people trying to eat right face two problems:

1. **They don't know what's in their food.** Menus don't show protein. Portions are inconsistent. Prices change at checkout.
2. **Tracking is fragmented.** They order food on one app, log calories on another, track workouts on a third. Nothing talks to each other.

Honest Meals solves both. We cook and deliver accurate, macro-counted meals — then give you the platform to track everything that matters.

---

## Brand Promise

**Honest.** Every price, every gram, every calorie — visible upfront. No dark patterns.

**High-Protein.** Every meal is built around real, substantial protein portions. Not garnish protein. Not marketing protein.

**Air Fried.** Not deep fried. Not compromised by oil. The same food, made cleaner.

**Complete.** We're not just the meal. We're the tracking, the workout log, the streak that keeps you consistent.

## Our Audience

People who:
- Train seriously or are working toward a body composition goal
- Want food that works with their numbers, not against them
- Are tired of calculating calorie estimates from vague menus
- Want data — actual data — on their body over time

They are not casual eaters looking for convenience. They are intentional about what they put in and how they move. We serve them.

## Platform Features

### 1. Meal Ordering

Our menu is not broad. It is correct.

**Vegetarian** — High-fiber, high-protein plant-based meals. Air fried. Starting at ₹60.

**Non-Vegetarian** — Premium protein cuts. Chicken, egg-based meals engineered for macros. Starting at ₹85.

**Elixirs** — Zero sugar detox drinks and energy blends. No syrups. No compromise.

**Custom Meals** — Build your own plate. Choose your ingredients, your portions, your macros.

Every meal on the platform includes:
- Calories
- Protein (grams)
- Carbohydrates (grams)
- Fat (grams)
- Fiber (grams)
- Cooking time
- Spice level
- Availability status

Pricing is transparent. Market cost + a visible service fee. What you see on the menu is what you pay at checkout.

---

### 2. Health Dashboard

A personal nutrition control panel. Not a food diary. A real-time view of whether you are hitting your goals today.

**What it tracks:**

| Metric | Detail |
|---|---|
| Calories | Daily intake vs. your set goal |
| Protein | Grams consumed vs. daily protein goal |
| Carbohydrates | Grams consumed vs. daily carb goal |
| Fat | Grams consumed vs. daily fat goal |
| Water | Intake logged in ml vs. daily hydration goal |
| Streak | Consecutive days you hit your targets |
| Longest streak | Your personal best |
| Current weight | Logged body weight |
| Goal weight | Set target |
| Starting weight | Baseline on day one |
| BMI | Calculated from height and weight |
| Height | User profile data |

**Food logging:**
- Log any meal from the Honest Meals menu — macros are auto-filled
- Manual food logging with custom fields
- Logs are tied to the current date automatically

**Goals:**
- Each user sets daily calorie, protein, and water goals
- Goals are active per-user and update the dashboard in real time

---

### 3. Workout Logger

Not a generic fitness tracker. A proper log for people who train with intent.

**What you can log:**
- Date
- Category (Push, Pull, Legs, Upper Body, Lower Body, Full Body, Cardio, HIIT, Yoga, Core, Flexibility, Endurance, Swimming, Cycling, Running, Boxing, CrossFit, Calisthenics, Olympic Lifting, Powerlifting, and custom)
- Duration (minutes)
- Intensity level
- Notes

**Per exercise:**
- Exercise name
- Sets
- Reps per set
- Weight (kg) per set

Every session is stored with full history. You can look back at what you lifted three months ago. That is the data that makes progression real.

---

### 4. Progress Tracking

Where all the data becomes a story.

- Weight over time — charted, logged with dates
- BMI over time — calculated automatically from every weight entry
- Streak history — visual representation of consistency
- Food log history — what you ate, when

You do not need to remember if you were improving. The data tells you.

---

### 5. Gymna — AI Health Coach

An AI assistant built into the platform. Not a chatbot. A plan generator.

**Diet Plan Generator:**
Answers 8 questions (dietary preference, goal, weight, height, activity level, allergies, cuisine preference, meals per day) → generates a structured day-by-day meal plan with:
- Full daily macro targets (calories, protein, carbs, fat)
- Meal-by-meal breakdown with food items, quantities, and macros
- Hydration recommendations
- Supplement guidance
- Dietary guidelines

**Workout Plan Generator:**
Answers 7 questions → generates a structured weekly training schedule with:
- Day-by-day workout split
- Exercise list per session with sets, reps, and rest periods
- Target muscles per day
- Warmup and cooldown protocols
- Session duration

Plans are saved to the user's account and accessible anytime.

---

### 6. Admin Panel

Internal tooling for managing the platform.

- Add, edit, and delete meals with full macro data and images
- Toggle meal availability
- Mark meals as featured
- Role-based access — only admins can access

## Design Principles

**No clutter.** One thing per screen. The user came to do something specific — don't distract them.

**Data first.** Numbers are the product. Show them clearly. Never approximate.

**Cream and contrast.** Warm ivory (`#F5F4EF`) base. Stone and lime. Dark panels for emphasis. The design should feel like a nutritional label — clean, trustworthy, readable.

**Mobile native.** Most users are on their phone between workouts or at meal time. Everything works at 390px first.

**No dark patterns.** No upsell traps. No hidden fees revealed at checkout. No confusing cancellation flows. Honest in the product the same way we're honest in the food.

---

## Voice and Tone

**Direct.** Say what you mean. "38g protein" not "protein-packed."

**Confident.** We know what we're building. No hedging.

**Not corporate.** This is not a startup trying to sound like a Fortune 500 company. It's a Pune brand talking directly to people who train.

**Never hype.** No "revolutionary," no "game-changing," no "best-in-class." Just what it is, said clearly.

### In practice:

| Don't say | Say instead |
|---|---|
| "Fuel your fitness journey" | "Food that works as hard as you" |
| "We're passionate about nutrition" | "Every gram is logged. Every price is real." |
| "Packed with protein" | "38g protein per serving" |
| "Transparency is our core value" | "No hidden fees. What you see is what you pay." |
| "Empowering your health goals" | "Watch the number move." |
